# Assentry — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Member or patient

- As a member, I want to be asked for access to exactly the information an answer needs, at the moment I ask the question, so that consent is a decision I can understand rather than a document I accepted last year.
- As a member, I want to revoke access in one action and see what I will lose, so that changing my mind is a real option and not a support ticket.
- As a member, I want my behavioural health information kept out of my assistant entirely unless I say otherwise, so that a benefits question can never surface something I did not choose to discuss.
- As a member, I want a cost or benefits answer to tell me when it was last verified, so that I can tell the difference between a current number and a stale one.
- As a member using a shared speaker, I want sensitive answers withheld from the room and offered privately, so that my household does not learn something from my assistant.
- As a member, I want a readable history of what my assistant used and told me, so that I can check it rather than trust it.
- As a member, I want to dispute an answer that turned out to be wrong and get a response based on what was actually said, so that being misinformed has a remedy.

### Digital product manager

- As a product manager, I want to see which purposes and data categories my channel is approved for before I design a flow, so that I stop building features legal will reject.
- As a product manager, I want the assistant to degrade gracefully when a grant is missing, so that an ungranted member gets a useful narrower answer instead of an error.
- As a product manager, I want to launch a new capability by requesting a purpose approval rather than a legal review of my code, so that release cadence is not set by the review calendar.

### Privacy and compliance officer

- As a privacy officer, I want every data use tied to a grant, a purpose, and a channel in an auditable record, so that I can answer a regulator with evidence instead of policy documents.
- As a privacy officer, I want to change a policy — narrow a scope, retire a purpose, suspend a channel — and have it take effect on the next request without a product release, so that control is real rather than advisory.
- As a compliance officer, I want steering disclosures enforced wherever curation presents economically interested options, so that a commercial preference does not become an undisclosed inducement.
- As a compliance officer, I want retention and deletion evidenced per category and channel, so that a deletion commitment is a verifiable fact.

### Member services agent

- As an agent, I want to see what the assistant told this member and the basis for it before I take the call, so that I am not contradicting my own organisation.
- As an agent, I want a one-step route to open a dispute with the interaction attached, so that a member's complaint is captured with its evidence rather than as free text.

### Platform administrator

- As an administrator, I want a channel suspended immediately when its approval lapses or its recipient agreement expires, and in-flight sessions moved to a compliant fallback, so that an expired instrument cannot keep processing member data.
